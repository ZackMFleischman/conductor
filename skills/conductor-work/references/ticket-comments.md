# Ticket comments and bounded context

Ticket comments are the existing append-only `ticket note` history. Use them for decisions and rationale, actionable questions and answers, blockers, handoffs, corrections, and material evidence that a future worker needs. Routine claim/state changes belong in activity; do not duplicate them as comments. Do not post heartbeats, unchanged status, repeated plans, or narration of each tool call.

Keep a comment to one short paragraph or a few focused bullets when possible. State what changed, why it matters, and the relevant evidence or next action. Link longer artifacts with a short summary of the exact fact they support. Brevity must not omit required acceptance evidence, failure reports, corrections, or a useful handoff. Preserve prior observations; append a correction rather than rewriting history.

Keep the current plan and requirements in the ticket description and the delivered result in its summary/evidence/QA fields, using existing authorized edit/submit operations. Comments record the discussion and history behind that current state. If a newer comment contradicts approved current fields, preserve the finding and resolve it through the applicable authorized update/review process; do not silently treat the comment as approval. A comment does not authorize scope, change ticket state, replace a required problem report, or satisfy a separate validation gate. Record systemic workflow problems with `problem add` and subsequent evidence with `problem append`; link the report or remedy from a ticket only when it helps that ticket's work.

Read the current ticket and the relevant recent comments when needed to resolve a decision, question, blocker, correction, or handoff. Do not fetch or replay the whole comment history automatically at startup, each poll, or each tool call. Retain the last relevant event ID/sequence in a checkpoint and inspect new relevant entries after that point when the available read surface supports it. Refresh ticket state/revision when required for ownership or a mutation; this does not require retelling every returned event.

The shipped `ticket show ID --json` includes at most 20 recent events of all kinds and `events_truncated`; it is not a complete comments log or a paginated CLI. Do not invent a `ticket comments` command or cursor flags. The read-only portal's Comments section loads 20 note entries at a time, with Load older on demand. If the bounded read lacks a specific decision you need, follow its cited artifact or use an available supported history read, and report the precise missing context if it remains unavailable. Do not assume an absent older comment means no decision was made.

Examples: "Chose X because Y; evidence: [artifact]." "Blocked on X; tried Y; next action Z." "Correction to the earlier result: X failed under Y; updated evidence: [artifact]." Avoid "Still working", "No changes", or copying a transcript into a note.
